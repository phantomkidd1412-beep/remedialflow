RemedialFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026

Who we are

RemedialFlow is a business app operated by Network Survey Ltd ("we", "us"). Our staff and contracted installers use it to record, review and export evidence of remediation work on properties. It is not for consumers. Accounts are created only by our administrators.

For data protection purposes, Network Survey Ltd is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: info@networksurveyltd.uk.

Information we collect

Account and sign-in: your name, work email address and role (admin, employee or installer). We store a hashed (one-way encrypted) password, not the password itself. We also keep sign-in session records. If a sign-in attempt fails, your IP address is held briefly to protect accounts from repeated password guessing.

Installer payments: pay type, job rates, earnings and payment records entered by our office.

Project and property information: property addresses and postcodes, project references, and inspection reports with their non-compliance (NC) descriptions and reference photos. Some of this information relates to the property's occupiers or owners.

Photos, videos and notes: evidence you capture or upload in the app, with the capture date, time and time zone, and file details.

Location: if you allow location access, the app records the GPS position (latitude, longitude and accuracy) when evidence is captured with the in-app camera. This is used to confirm where evidence was taken, and the position may be printed on evidence images. Location is never collected in the background, and the app still works if you refuse it.

Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store your device's push notification token so we can send you work alerts.

Activity records: an audit log of actions in the app, such as uploads, reviews, approvals and exports, so that compliance records can be checked.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use information

To run the remediation workflow: assigning work, receiving and reviewing evidence, and producing evidence packs (PDF and ZIP) for compliance bodies such as TrustMark.

To check evidence automatically with AI (see below), and to calculate installer payments.

To send password-reset emails and in-app or push notifications about work that needs your attention.

To keep the service secure and to keep accurate compliance records.

[CONFIRM] Legal basis: we process this information because it is necessary for our contracts with installers and clients, and for our legitimate interest in providing auditable remediation evidence.

AI evidence assessment

Evidence photos, frames taken from videos, the NC descriptions and installer notes are sent to Google's Gemini AI model, through our platform provider's AI service, to check whether they show the remedial work. Property addresses and names are not included in these requests. AI results are advice only: staff review and approve them, and safety-critical items always need a person to sign them off.

Where information is stored and who processes it

Dropbox (Dropbox, Inc.): evidence photos, videos and evidence-pack exports are stored in our company Dropbox account.

Emergent (our app platform provider): hosts the app's server and database. It also provides the services we use for AI requests to Google Gemini, password-reset email and push notifications. Evidence uploaded before we moved to Dropbox may still be held in Emergent's file storage.

Google (Gemini AI): processes evidence for the AI assessment described above.

Apple and Google push services: deliver notifications to your device.

We share evidence packs with compliance bodies such as TrustMark when we submit them. We do not sell personal data.

[CONFIRM] Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on their standard contractual safeguards.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Access is limited by role: installers only see the work assigned to them, and evidence files are only available to signed-in, authorised users through time-limited links. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

How long we keep information

There is currently no automatic deletion schedule. Work evidence, reviews and audit history are kept as compliance records for the properties concerned until our administrators delete them. Account details are kept while your account is active.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app at any time: Account (or Profile) → Delete account, then confirm with your password. Our administrators can also delete an account for you.

Deleting your account permanently removes your name, email address, password, sign-in sessions and notification registration. Evidence you submitted stays as part of the property's compliance record, labelled "Deleted user". To ask for anything else to be removed, email info@networksurveyltd.uk.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. Email info@networksurveyltd.uk to do this. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

RemedialFlow is a workplace app and is not intended for children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date below and, where appropriate, tell users in the app.
